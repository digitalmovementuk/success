Botox or dermal fillers — which do you need?

People often ask about Botox and dermal fillers together, so it helps to be clear that they solve different problems. Botox relaxes the muscles behind movement lines — forehead, frown and crow's feet — to soften them. Fillers replace lost volume or refine shape, for example cheeks, lips or deeper folds. Many people benefit from one, some from a thoughtful combination, and plenty from neither just yet.

Rather than selling you both, our doctors will tell you honestly what — if anything — suits your goals. A good way to think about it is movement versus volume: if the concern is lines that appear when you move, Botox is usually the answer; if it is flatness or loss of shape, fillers may suit better. Whatever you are considering, the free consultation is the place to work it out, with no pressure to commit on the day.
