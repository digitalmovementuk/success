# Puriva Aesthetic Clinic — 400-Page Exposure & Growth Plan

**Today: 40 pages → target footprint: 400 pages** (~10% there).

## The model
- **32 services × 12 locations = 384 targeted pages** + 44 service/location hubs = **428 addressable pages**.
- DM builds toward ~**400** of these — every service, across every relevant location.

## Phased build
| Phase | Scope | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — money core | 8 services × 6 locations | **48 pages** |
| Phase 2 — widen locations | 8 services × 12 locations | **96 pages** |
| Phase 3 — full service map | 32 services × 6 locations | **192 pages** |
| Phase 4 — complete matrix + hubs | 32 services × 12 locations | **384 pages** |

## Illustrative qualified-lead reach (NOT a guarantee)
~**16–96 enquiries/month** at maturity — assumes 2–6 qualified visits/page/mo and a 2–4% enquiry rate on high-intent local pages, with pages passing the relevance + quality gates. Actual results depend on execution, competition and demand.

## Why ~400 pages
- Appear exactly where intent is (service + location), not a generic homepage.
- ~85%% low-volume but high-conversion, ready-to-book searches — hundreds of them.
- Compounding topical authority + AI-search citations.
- A defensive moat: every page you own is one a competitor can't.
