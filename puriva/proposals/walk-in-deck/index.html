SEO · Organic

Scale your business without ads

A permanent asset on your balance sheet. Every page you build keeps earning year after year.

Paid Ads · Meta + Google

Short-term digital marketing tactic only

Useful for launches and seasonal pushes. Stops working the day you stop paying.

  • No build-up. Every visitor costs the same — there's no asset that gets cheaper over time
