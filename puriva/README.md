- Growth proposal links to the audit, exposure plan, location pages, retainer calendar, research and worksheet re-pointed to `../../seo/`.
- First-look teaser: its seven referenced images copied to `proposals/first-look/assets/`, references updated.
- Local absolute path prefixes stripped from gate-report and research data (page paths now relative, e.g. `pages/botox-seminyak.html`).
- Nothing else in the content was edited.
